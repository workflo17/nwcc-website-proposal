Now Enrolling: Summer Music Classes at NWCC!

nwcf1987 · Apr 28 · 1 min read

At Northwest Community Center, our mission is to promote community development through multicultural and multilingual programs. We believe that music is a universal language that brings people together, and we are excited to invite you to join our 2026 Summer Music Program! Whether you are looking to start a new hobby or sharpen your existing skills, our Saturday classes offer a supportive environment for students of all levels. Find Your Rhythm: our program covers a wide range of instruments.

First Day of 2026 Education & Culture Classes

nwcf1987 · Apr 20 · 3 min read

This past Saturday marked the exciting start of the 2026 Next Generation Culture and Education Classes. The day was filled with energy and curiosity as students gathered to explore new skills and knowledge in Art & Calligraphy, English, Taekwondo, and Math. These classes offer a unique blend of cultural enrichment and academic growth, designed to engage young learners in meaningful ways. A Day Full of Learning and Discovery: the first day set a positive tone for the year ahead.

2026 Summer Camp Registration!

nwcf1987 · Mar 25 · 1 min read

TACOMA, WA. It’s the season we’ve all been waiting for. The Northwest Community Center (NWCC) is proud to announce the return of our Annual Summer Saturday Program! Since our founding, the Summer Academy has been a cornerstone for Tacoma families, providing a vibrant bridge between the school years. We are back for 2026 with an even bigger lineup of academics, athletics, and cultural arts. The 2026 Schedule: Mark Your Calendars. Our 2026 season officially kicks off this spring.
