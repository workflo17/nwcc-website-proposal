Nondiscrimination Policy

NW Community Centers (NWCC) does not, by policy or practice, discriminate against any person or group on the basis of race, color, religion, gender, sexual orientation, national origin, age, disability, gender identity and expression, marital or military status, or based on any individual’s status in any group or class protected by applicable federal, state, or local law.

This policy applies to the people we serve and to everyone who takes part in our programs, events and services. It also covers how NWCC recruits, hires, and treats its employees, contractors, volunteers, and board members.

No one is required to hold or accept any religious or other belief to take part in NWCC’s programs or to receive its services.

Anyone with a question or concern about this policy may contact NWCC at nwcf1987@gmail.com or (253) 468-8539.

Adopted by the Board of Directors on Oct 09, 2026.
Kyongsuk An, President
