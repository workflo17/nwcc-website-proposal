About NW Community Centers

NW Community Centers (NWCC), formerly Northwest Culture Foundation, is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit in Pierce County, Washington (EIN 91-2025901). Our mission is to promote community development through multicultural and multilingual programs. Established in 1987, NWCC serves as a hub for cultural exchange for immigrants, refugees, and low-income families. We focus on multi-ethnic education and culture, and more than 80% of our programs are offered free of charge.

Performing arts, every week

Our cultural program is designed to foster an environment where individuals can understand, share, and learn about other countries' customs and cultures. By participating in our program, you can immerse yourself in diverse cultural experiences, including traditional music, dance, food, art, and customs.

We regularly organize multi-ethnic performances, known as the Friendship Festival. The next one is the 2027 K‑12 Children and Youth Friendship Festival, Saturday, April 24, 2027.

Why we need your support

More than 80% of our programs are offered free of charge. One of our core goals is to ensure that children from low-income families have equitable access to multicultural learning and high-quality education.
