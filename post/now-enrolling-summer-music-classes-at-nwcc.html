Now Enrolling: Summer Music Classes at NWCC!

April 28, 2026

These classes began July 11, 2026.

At Northwest Community Center, our mission is to promote community development through multicultural and multilingual programs. We believe that music is a universal language that brings people together, and we are excited to invite you to join our 2026 Summer Music Program!

Whether you are looking to start a new hobby or sharpen your existing skills, our Saturday classes offer a supportive environment for students of all levels.

Find Your Rhythm

Our program covers a wide range of instruments to ensure every student finds their perfect match. Classes currently offered include:

  • Piano & Keyboard
  • Violin & Flute
  • Drums & Guitar
  • General Music Classes

Program Details at a Glance

Start date
July 11th
Schedule
Every Saturday from 12:00 PM to 1:00 PM
Location
New Life Culture Center, 9702 B St E, Tacoma
Eligibility
Open to ages 7 years through Adult
Tuition
$100 for 4 lessons per month (Group lessons based on 4 students)

How to Register

The deadline to secure your spot is June 30th. You can text 253-468-8539 with any questions.

Why Choose NWCC?

More than 80% of our programs are offered free of charge to ensure equitable access to high-quality education. While our specialized music classes require tuition to cover professional instruction and materials, your participation helps sustain a vibrant, creative community here in Tacoma.

Don’t wait, let’s make some noise this summer!
