2026 Summer Camp Registration!

March 25, 2026

The 2026 season ended September 19.

TACOMA, WA. It’s the season we’ve all been waiting for. The Northwest Community Center (NWCC) is proud to announce the return of our Annual Summer Saturday Program! Since our founding, the Summer Academy has been a cornerstone for Tacoma families, providing a vibrant bridge between the school years. We are back for 2026 with an even bigger lineup of academics, athletics, and cultural arts.

The 2026 Schedule: Mark Your Calendars

Our 2026 season officially kicks off this spring and runs through the heart of the summer.

April 18, 2026 to September 19, 2026
