Programs

Our program offers a wide range of classes to cater to various interests and needs. Students can benefit from a well-rounded education that includes math, reading, STEM, sports, arts & crafts, and language and culture classes. Whether a student is inclined towards academia, athletics, or the arts, our program provides a holistic educational experience that fosters growth and development in multiple areas.

  • World Language/Culture

    Our language and culture classes will cover different countries and cultures in every session. Your child will gain experience in cultural differences and conversational language.

  • Math

    Our math program offers comprehensive K‑12 math classes, including fundamental concepts and basic algebra and calculus. Students develop critical thinking and problem-solving skills, fostering a strong foundation for future academic and professional success.

  • Reading & Storytelling

    Our K‑3 reading and storytelling program nurtures a love for reading while enhancing literacy skills through engaging storytelling sessions and interactive activities. We aim to build confidence and enthusiasm for reading in young learners, laying a solid foundation for their academic journey.

  • STEM

    STEM programs for K‑8 grade children offer hands-on experiences that foster creativity, critical thinking, and problem-solving skills. These programs prepare students for future careers in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics while developing essential skills such as logical reasoning, innovation, and teamwork. The Next Generation STEM Club

  • Arts & Crafts

    Art and craft classes for K‑12 students offer numerous benefits, including promoting creativity, self-expression, and the development of fine motor skills. These classes provide a therapeutic outlet for children to express emotions and foster a sense of accomplishment through their artistic creations. Overall, art and craft classes contribute to well-rounded development and holistic learning for K‑12 students.

  • Sports

    Our youth sports program offers soccer, golf, football, volleyball, tennis, and fitness sessions. Engaging in these sports promotes physical fitness, teamwork, discipline, and sportsmanship. Children can discover their passion for sports, develop new skills, and build lasting friendships in a fun and supportive environment.

Culture & Performing Arts

Our cultural program is designed to foster an environment where individuals can understand, share, and learn about other countries' customs and cultures. By participating in our program, you can immerse yourself in diverse cultural experiences, including traditional music, dance, food, art, and customs. Whether you are a child, a teenager, or an adult, there are specific programs tailored to your age group, ensuring that everyone can engage and learn in a meaningful and appropriate way for their stage of life.

The performing arts program offers a range of activities, including fine arts, crafts, music, and pop dance. The program aims to engage in community performances and competitions. We regularly organize multi-ethnic performances, known as the Friendship Festival.

Outreach Programs / Global Exchange

The Outreach Global Exchange Program creates opportunities for individuals to contribute to community economic development. The program aims to cultivate talent by offering short-term internships and travel programs in the United States. Additionally, the program supports local businesses and communities by providing accommodations, food, transportation, and school tuition for program participants. Furthermore, the program also aims to send American students or job seekers overseas to provide them with opportunities to grow into future leaders through international cooperation.

The Outreach program is in collaboration with the U.S. Department of State J-1 Exchange Program.
