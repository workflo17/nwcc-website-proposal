Next Generation STEM Club

The "Next Generation STEM Club" is a free STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics) program offered by NW Community Centers for children ranging from kindergarten through 12th grade.

From February to August 2027, a total of 40 students will be divided into four age-based groups (Kindergarten to 1st grade, 2nd to 5th grade, 6th to 8th grade, and 9th to 12th grade) to participate in 24 sessions (each lasting 4 hours, held approximately once a week). Six instructors will lead lessons covering Arduino programming for robotics (fostering skills in computer science, technology, and engineering), Scratch coding, and arithmetic using the abacus (soroban). Following the 2026 program, which saw 20 participants, we are expanding the scale for 2027 to provide learning opportunities to even more children, addressing the high demand from parents who were previously unable to enroll their children.

Why STEM

STEM programs for K‑12 grade children offer hands-on experiences that foster creativity, critical thinking, and problem-solving skills. These programs prepare students for future careers in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics while developing essential skills such as logical reasoning, innovation, and teamwork.
